Banking & Finance — Caribbean

Real-Time Fraud Detection for a Leading Caribbean Bank

Challenge

One of the Caribbean's largest commercial banks was experiencing a sharp rise in card-not-present and synthetic identity fraud, with legacy rule-based systems flagging fewer than 58% of fraudulent transactions and generating false positives that eroded customer trust across its Jamaica, Trinidad, and Barbados operations.

Solution

We deployed a real-time ML fraud detection pipeline using gradient boosting and graph neural networks, integrated directly into the bank's transaction authorization flow with sub-30ms inference latency across all island markets.

Telecommunications — Caribbean

Churn Prediction & Retention Automation for a Caribbean Multi-Island Carrier

Challenge

A major Caribbean telecommunications provider operating across 12 island markets was losing 8.2% of subscribers annually to competitors, with no predictive capability to identify at-risk customers and no automated retention workflow in place — a critical gap in a market where switching costs are low.

Solution

We built a churn prediction model scoring subscribers weekly across all island markets and integrated it with a CRM-based retention workflow that automatically triggered personalized offers for high-risk customers, segmented by island and service tier.
