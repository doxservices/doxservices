About DoxServices

We are a global technology consulting firm helping the world's largest organizations navigate digital transformation with intelligence, precision, and discretion.

The Caribbean's Partner in AI-Powered Transformation

DoxServices was founded on a simple premise: enterprises across the Caribbean and beyond deserve a consulting partner that combines the analytical rigor of a top-tier global strategy firm with the technical depth of a world-class engineering organization.

Our roots run deep across the region. We have built trusted relationships with some of the Caribbean's most respected financial institutions, telecommunications providers, energy companies, and government enterprises — across Jamaica, Trinidad & Tobago, Barbados, the Cayman Islands, Guyana, and beyond. We understand the unique regulatory landscape, market dynamics, and competitive pressures that define Caribbean business.

Over more than a decade of engagements, we have helped leading Caribbean banks automate compliance and detect fraud, regional telecom operators cut churn, healthcare systems accelerate research, and energy companies slash unplanned downtime — and we bring that same intelligence to enterprises worldwide.

We operate with complete confidentiality. Our clients trust us with their most sensitive data and strategic initiatives, and we take that responsibility seriously.
